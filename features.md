# 🎓 Campus Buddy — Your Smart Campus Companion

**Campus Buddy** is a student-centric campus assistant designed to simplify college life by bringing essential academic information, campus resources, and student collaboration tools together in one platform.

Our goal is to help students stay organized, connected, and informed throughout their academic journey.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 1. Student Dashboard
- Provides a centralized overview of important academic information.
- Displays upcoming assignments, deadlines, announcements, and reminders.
- Helps students quickly access frequently used features.

### 📝 2. Assignment & Deadline Management
- View and organize academic assignments.
- Track upcoming submission deadlines.
- Reduce the chances of missing important academic tasks.

### 📅 3. Timetable & Academic Schedule
- Access class schedules in one place.
- Keep track of lectures and academic activities.
- Help students manage their time more effectively.

### 🔔 4. Smart Notifications & Announcements
- Keep students informed about important college updates.
- Highlight upcoming deadlines and relevant announcements.
- Reduce dependency on scattered communication channels.

### 🧭 5. Campus Navigation
- Help students locate classrooms, departments, laboratories, offices, and other campus facilities.
- Make it easier for new students to become familiar with the campus.
- Reduce confusion when searching for unfamiliar locations.

### 🤝 6. Peer Connect
- Encourage collaboration and communication among students.
- Support peer-to-peer learning and academic discussions.
- Help students connect with classmates and exchange useful information.

### 📚 7. Student Resources
- Provide a centralized place for useful academic resources and study materials.
- Make important student information easier to find.
- Reduce the time spent searching across multiple platforms.

### 🎯 8. Student-Centric Experience
- Designed around common student challenges and needs.
- Aims to provide a simple, accessible, and intuitive user experience.
- Brings essential campus utilities together instead of requiring students to switch between multiple platforms.

## 💡 Problem We Aim to Solve

Students often rely on multiple applications, messaging groups, websites, and noticeboards to manage their academic responsibilities and access campus information.

This fragmented experience can lead to missed deadlines, overlooked announcements, difficulty navigating campus facilities, and reduced opportunities for collaboration.

**Campus Buddy aims to bring these essential student services together in one convenient platform.**

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify everyday academic management.
- Improve access to campus information and resources.
- Help students stay organized and informed.

- Improve the overall campus experience through a student-focused digital solution.

Campus Buddy was developed as part of a Design Thinking project at **MIT Academy of Engineering (MITAOE), Alandi, Pune**.

The project follows a student-centered approach, beginning with the identification of student problems and progressing toward ideation, prototyping, and product development.
